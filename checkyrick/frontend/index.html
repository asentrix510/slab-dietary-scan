Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the label scanning? +

We use state-of-the-art vision OCR to transcribe text even from wrinkled packaging, curved bottles, and low-light photos. The extracted ingredients are then verified against live food safety databases.

Can I customize my personal allergies and restrictions? +

Yes! Under your account profile, you can set custom allergy matrices (peanuts, dairy, shellfish, etc.), religious guidelines (Halal, Kosher, Jain), or medical diets (Celiac, FODMAP, Vegan). Every scan automatically applies your saved profile.

Where do the regulatory citations come from? +

We fetch live verification from authoritative sources including the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), US Food & Drug Administration (FDA), and World Health Organization (WHO).

Is DietXplore free to use? +

Yes, the core scanner and dietary profile features are completely free to use for personal dietary safety.
